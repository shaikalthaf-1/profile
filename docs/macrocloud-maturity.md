## Approved Language Rules

Permitted after the maturity statement itself is approved:

- `Shipped`, only for cloud engineering and consulting services
- `Venture`
- `In development`
- `Research preview`
- `Prototype`
- `Roadmap`
- `Building`
- `Focused on`
- `Designed for`, when describing intent rather than a proven result

Required public distinctions:

- `Cloud engineering and consulting engagements` is permitted; `platform customers` is not.
- Azure may be emphasized as engineering expertise while the platform integration remains labeled `Prototype`.
- Do not describe MacroCloud as a generally available `multi-cloud platform` while AWS and GCP integrations remain `Roadmap`.
- Capability labels must appear next to the relevant feature, not only in a disclaimer elsewhere on the page.

Not permitted without additional evidence:

- `Market leader`
- `Platform launch`
- `Chosen by organizations`
- `Enterprise-grade` as a factual certification or operating result
- `Production-ready`
- `Trusted by`
- `Platform customers`, customer counts, or customer testimonials
- `Global offices`, compliance certifications, or formal partnerships
- Performance, availability, savings, deployment, or supported-service metrics
