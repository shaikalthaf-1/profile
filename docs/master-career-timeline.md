## Verification Standard

A record becomes `Verified` only when the owner confirms every required field and identifies supporting evidence such as an employment letter, contract, payslip, experience certificate, public employer profile, or approved resume. Evidence should not be committed to this public repository if it contains personal or confidential information.

Required fields:

- Organization name and approved public spelling.
- Exact public role title.
- Start month and year.
- End month and year, or `Present`.
- Employment type.
- Location and work mode.
- Public description.
- Whether the organization/client may be named.
