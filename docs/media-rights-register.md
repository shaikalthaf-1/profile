# Media and Publication Rights Register

Status: Publication-rights policy and current-asset disposition approved by owner on 2026-07-18. Assets without documented rights remain excluded.

This register covers headshots, logos, icons, architecture diagrams, screenshots, certificates, video, and other visual evidence. Public availability is not proof of ownership or permission.

## Rights Status

- `Approved public`: ownership or license is documented and the intended use is approved.
- `Permission required`: ownership, license, trademark use, or subject consent is incomplete.
- `Internal only`: must not be published.
- `Replace`: unsuitable because provenance, security, reliability, or rights cannot be established.

## Approved Publication Policy

### Approved for Public Use

- Owner-controlled photographs of Shaik Althaf.
- MacroCloud and PROSKILL IT Technologies logos supplied or approved by their owner.
- Architecture diagrams, illustrations, icons, and animations created by the owner for publication.
- Properly licensed third-party icon sets with the license and version recorded.
- Sanitized screenshots from owner-controlled products and public GitHub repositories.
- Certification badges where the issuer permits the intended use.

Approval remains conditional on security review, accurate context, accessible alternatives, and an optimized local source file.

### Requires Verification Before Use

- Employer, client, conference, or event logos and photographs.
- Third-party illustrations and stock photography.
- Product screenshots containing customer, employee, or environment information.
- Certification badges without confirmed issuer terms.

### Do Not Publish

- Internal dashboards, presentations, documents, or architecture diagrams.
- Client-owned or employer-owned source material.
- Teams meeting screenshots or employee photographs without consent.
- Customer Azure Portal or other cloud-console screenshots.
- Production configurations, identifiers, customer data, credentials, or secrets.

## Publication Checklist

Before an asset becomes `Approved public`, record privately or in a non-sensitive project note:

1. Creator and copyright owner.
2. License or written permission.
3. Trademark and organization-naming approval where applicable.
4. Consent from identifiable people.
5. Security and confidentiality review.
6. Approved crop, edit, and context.
7. Alt text or textual equivalent.
8. Optimized format, dimensions, and local delivery path.
9. Approval date and reviewer.
10. Expiration or revocation conditions, if any.

Phase 2 may use only assets marked `Approved public` or newly created assets whose ownership or license is recorded before use. All other current assets must be omitted or replaced.
